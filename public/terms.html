What this is

MAQLAB is a free multiplayer party game, playable in a browser or as a Discord Activity. By playing it you accept these terms. If you do not, please do not play.

Accounts

Playing requires signing in with Discord. Your name in the game is your Discord display name — there is no name to type, so nobody can sit down as someone else. Signing in also gives you a profile, a place on the leaderboard and the ability to follow other players. You are responsible for what happens under your account.

How to behave

This is a game played with friends. Do not:

  • harass, insult or target other players
  • use an offensive or impersonating display name
  • cheat, exploit bugs, or automate play to inflate your score
  • try to break, overload or gain unauthorised access to the service
  • submit illegal or abusive content through suggestions or reports

Moderation

We may remove a player from a room, reset a profile's progress, or ban an account that breaks these rules — with or without notice. Reports are reviewed by the game's administrators.

Suggestions and reports you submit may be read, stored and acted on. Do not send anything confidential. Ideas you send may be used to improve the game without any obligation to you.

Nothing is for sale

There are no payments, purchases or subscriptions. Scores, levels and achievements have no monetary value and cannot be traded or cashed out.

السلوك

هذي لعبة بين ربع. ممنوع:

  • التنمّر على اللاعبين أو إهانتهم أو استهدافهم

  • الغش أو استغلال الأخطاء البرمجية أو أتمتة اللعب لتضخيم نقاطك
